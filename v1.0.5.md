# FiMa v1.0.5

Release date: 2026-08-24

## New

- **Snapshots** — turn on **Snapshot on Close** in Settings and FiMa saves your whole finances as a single, standalone `snapshot.html` each time you close it. It opens in any browser, needs no internet, and you navigate between pages and drill into individual accounts, budgets, investments, debts and assets just like in the app — handy for sharing, printing, or keeping a dated record outside FiMa.
  - **Snapshot options** in Settings — choose where the snapshot is saved, whether the file name starts with the database name and ends with the date and/or time, and whether every record is expanded so all children are visible.
- **Temporarily disable a transaction** — hover any transaction and hit the new **⊘** button to switch it off. The row greys out and its amount comes back out of the account total at the top of the list, so you can see what the balance would be without it — handy for hunting down a figure that looks wrong before deciding whether to correct or delete it. Hit **✓** to switch it back on.
  - Disabling never touches your database, and it only affects the transactions page — it's a scratch view, so the Accounts page, Dashboard and Balance Sheet still show the real balance.
  - Everything switches back on when you close FiMa, and editing a disabled transaction re-enables it automatically so the corrected amount counts again.
  - The adjusted total appears when you're viewing a single account; the header also tells you how many transactions are disabled and how much is being left out.

- **Reports page** — see how a budget actually performed instead of just what it planned. Pick a budget, choose a period, and FiMa lays your planned income and expense next to what really happened.
  - **Occurrences** — a monthly budget read over six months should plan for six months, so tell FiMa how many times the budget runs in the period and every planned figure scales with it. Defaults to 1.
  - **Two charts** — one plots income, expense and the plan for each period on its own, so a month that ran over stands out; the other plots the same figures as running totals, where the gap between the lines is your surplus or deficit building up. The period picks its own detail: up to two months is plotted per day, up to two years per month, and anything longer per year.
  - **Category performance** — every budgeted category and sub-category shows what you planned, what you actually spent, how much is left over or overdrawn, and how much of the plan you have used. Money left is green, going over is red.
  - **Drill into any line** — hover a row and hit the new **☰** button to see the individual transactions behind its figure, so a number that looks wrong can be traced to the transactions that caused it.
- **Exclude a transaction from budget reports** — some transactions are not really income or spending. Tick **Exclude from budget** when adding or editing one and reports will skip it, while your account balances stay exactly as they are.
  - **Transfers are excluded automatically** — moving money between your own accounts was never income or an expense, so both sides are left out from the start. Existing transfers are updated when you first open this version.
  - Excluded transactions are marked in the transactions list so you can see at a glance what reports are leaving out.

- **Progress on savings goals and asset replacements** — until now FiMa only told you what to put aside each month; it never knew whether you had. Nominate the category the money actually goes into and every goal and replacement plan grows a progress bar.
  - **Tracked Categories** in Settings — pick a **Savings Category** and an **Asset Replacement Category** (and, if you want to be more precise, a sub-category of it). Leave one blank to turn progress off for that page.
  - **The pot is shared out fairly** — FiMa takes everything in that category and divides it between your goals by what each one should have received by now, so money you saved before a goal existed is never handed to it, and a goal due sooner draws the larger share. A goal that has reached its target stops drawing and its share goes back to the goals still short.
  - Money **transferred** into the tracked category counts, so it makes no difference whether you record a contribution as a transfer into a savings account or as an ordinary transaction.
  - Each goal shows **what has been put toward it** next to its monthly figure, and opening one adds **Saved So Far**, **Remaining** and **Progress** to the breakdown. The total row at the top carries the whole pot and its own progress bar, and tells you if you have saved more than every goal combined needs.

- **Start Date on savings goals and replacement plans** — a goal used to count from the day you created it in FiMa, which is rarely the day you started saving. Set the **Start Date** yourself and the monthly figure is worked out over the real term.
  - Existing goals and plans take the day they were created, so nothing you already had changes.
