Everything in one place

One app for every part of your money

FiMa brings your accounts, spending, savings and long-term plans together — so you always know exactly where you stand.

Dashboard

See where you stand at a glance — your accounts, investments, debts and assets up top, with a live category breakdown beneath.

Balance sheet

Your complete net worth on one page — assets and liabilities side by side, each grouped (even good vs. bad debt) and expandable down to the items behind every total.

Reports

See how a budget actually performed — planned against actual income and expense over any period, charted month by month, with every category showing what was left over or overdrawn.

Accounts

Track every bank account, wallet and cash stash in one list, organised by the categories that make sense to you.

Transactions

Log money in and out, search and filter your history, and watch every balance update instantly across the app.

Budgets

Set budgets, see exactly where the money is going with a clear breakdown, and archive the ones you no longer need.

Investments

Follow your portfolio over time with history and growth graphs, and break holdings down by category and sub-category.

Debts

Keep loans and credit in check with payoff history and a graph that shows your balances trending down over time.

Assets

Record property, vehicles and valuables so the things you own are counted in your true net worth.

Asset replacement

Plan ahead for replacing the things that wear out, using inflation assumptions to estimate what they'll cost later — and track how far along each plan already is.

Savings goals

Set things aside for what matters and use return assumptions to project where you'll land. Nominate the category your contributions go into and FiMa shares it out across your goals, so every one carries a real progress bar.

Categories

Build your own categories and sub-categories with custom icons and colours, so FiMa mirrors the way you think about money.

Snapshots

Save your whole financial picture as a single standalone HTML file every time you close FiMa. It opens in any browser with no internet, and you navigate the pages and drill into individual records just like in the app — handy for sharing, printing or keeping a dated record.

Make it yours

Switch between light and dark themes, choose your currency symbol and date format, set inflation and return rates for projections, tell FiMa which categories fund your savings, and tune how snapshots are named and saved.

Backups you control

Your data lives in a single local database file. Back it up, move it, or point FiMa at a new one — all from Settings, in a click.

Built private by default

Your money is nobody's business but yours

FiMa is a desktop app, not a service. There's no sign-up, no server, and nothing to leak.

100% offline

Everything runs on your PC. FiMa never phones home and works perfectly with no internet connection.

Your data, one file

All your finances are stored in a local database on your machine. No cloud accounts, ever.

Free, no subscription

Download it, use it, keep it. There are no tiers, trials, ads or upsells.

Light & dark themes

A clean, modern interface that's easy on the eyes — switch themes whenever you like.

Your currency & dates

Set your own currency symbol and date format so FiMa speaks your language.

Plan for the future

Built-in inflation and expected-return settings power the projections across savings and asset replacement.
